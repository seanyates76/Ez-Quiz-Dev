Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 August 2026

Overview

EZ Quiz is a lightweight, installable web app that runs primarily on-device. We designed it to minimize the collection and processing of personal data. This policy explains what the app does locally, what limited serverless calls may occur, and your choices.

What We Don’t Collect

  • No account creation
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or ad networks
  • No storage of your quiz content on our servers

Data Processed Locally (On Your Device)

The app may store small settings in your browser’s storage to improve your experience:

  • Theme and UI preferences (e.g., dark/light mode, timer settings)
  • Progress state (in-memory while the app is open)

You can clear these at any time using the in‑app “Reset App” action or your browser’s site data controls.

Cookie note: A single, optional cookie may be used to remember display preferences (e.g., “Always show advanced options”). It stores a simple true/false value and expires after roughly one year.

Service Worker and Offline Use

EZ Quiz uses a service worker to cache the app shell (HTML/CSS/JS/icons) for offline access. Cached assets remain on your device until you clear site data or the app refreshes its cache during updates.

Optional AI Features

If you click “Create Quiz,” the app may call a serverless function that in turn calls a third‑party AI API (e.g., Google Gemini or OpenAI) to create quiz questions from your provided topic or study material, length, and options. If you click “Explain” in Results, the app may call a serverless function to explain the relevant quiz question using that question, answer choices, and correct-answer context. We forward only what’s necessary to perform each request. Responses are returned to your browser and are not persisted by us.

  • Third‑party processors: Google (Gemini) and/or OpenAI, depending on configuration.
  • Content transmitted: for quiz generation, your chosen topic or study material and generation parameters; for explanations, the relevant quiz question, answer choices, and correct-answer context. We do not include your prior quiz answers, selected learner answers, or local settings.
  • Retention by providers: subject to each provider’s policies. Review their privacy documentation if you use AI generation.

ChatGPT Plugin

When you use EZ Quiz inside ChatGPT, ChatGPT sends the EZ Quiz tool only the information needed for the action you requested. This may include a quiz topic, generation settings, study text you intentionally supplied, or quiz lines you asked us to open. EZ Quiz returns the generated or validated quiz to ChatGPT so it can be shown in the conversation and in the interactive quiz player.

  • Purpose: generate, validate, and display the quiz you requested.
  • Recipients: our hosting provider (Netlify), the configured AI provider when generation is requested, and OpenAI as the ChatGPT host.
  • Our retention: EZ Quiz does not create user accounts or save ChatGPT quiz requests in an application database. Hosting security logs and AI-provider processing may be retained under those providers’ policies.
  • Interactive state: current question and score may be held by the ChatGPT interface while the quiz is open. EZ Quiz does not use that state for advertising or profiling.

Do not submit passwords, API keys, government identifiers, payment-card information, protected health information, or other sensitive personal data. You can avoid server processing by using the standalone app with manually entered quiz lines, and you can remove a ChatGPT conversation using ChatGPT’s controls.

Feedback (Optional)

If you choose to send feedback using the in‑app panel, we collect only what you submit:

  • Message (required) and Email (optional) that you type into the form.
  • Basic rate‑limit data (IP address), used transiently to prevent spam/abuse. This is stored in memory for a short window and not persisted by us.

Your feedback is relayed via email to the maintainer’s inbox. We do not create a database of submissions, but the email copy may be retained in the maintainer’s mailbox. Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback.

Payments and External Links

The “☕ Support” link navigates to an external donations page (e.g., Buy Me a Coffee). Interactions there are governed by that site’s terms and privacy policy.

Children’s Privacy

EZ Quiz is a general‑audience app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information via a third‑party form or service linked from the app, contact us so we can assist.

Security

We follow reasonable practices for a static web app and serverless functions, including HTTPS, scoped API keys, and least‑privilege configuration. Because the app runs in your browser, the security of your device and browser also matters.

Your Choices

  • Use the app entirely offline with manual input (no AI calls)
  • Clear data via “Reset App” or by clearing your browser’s site storage
  • Decline to use external links or AI features

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves (for example, if we add providers or settings). We will revise the “Effective” date and publish the latest version here.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or concerns? Email ez.quizapp@gmail.com or use the support page.

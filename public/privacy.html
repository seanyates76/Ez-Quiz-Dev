When data leaves your computer

Create Quiz sends your topic, selected options, and relevant study text to your chosen provider through the local launcher. After a completed quiz, later requests also include a compact learning summary: missed topics, question types, and recent question text. Source material may be split into batches. Explain sends the selected question, choices, and correct-answer context, without learner answers. Load models uses your key to retrieve available model IDs without submitting study text.

PDF and image extraction asks before sending the file to the chosen provider. Gemini handles PDFs; image support depends on the selected model. Text and DOCX extraction do not call an AI provider.

The local launcher forwards requests over HTTPS to generativelanguage.googleapis.com or api.openai.com. It does not write keys or study content to disk, log request bodies, or route requests through an EZ Quiz cloud server. No provider fallback occurs. The provider sees your API key, request, and connection metadata and applies its own billing and data policies.

Your API key

Your key is saved in this browser’s localStorage on your device so you can use it next time. Uncheck Remember key before saving to keep it only for the current visit. Keys are not included in quiz exports or launcher logs. Browser storage is not an encrypted credential vault.

Forget key removes the saved key and clears it from this tab. Other open tabs may keep an in-memory copy until closed. Changing providers clears the previous key. To revoke a key, use your provider’s console.

Local access and offline use

The launcher binds to 127.0.0.1, checks the Host and Origin, requires a per-run request token, and serves only files under public/. Its content policy restricts the page’s network requests to the local origin. These controls do not protect a compromised device or malicious replacement of the application files.

Manual quiz practice works offline after the app shell has cached successfully. The local launcher is required for DOCX extraction and AI calls. Cloud AI additionally needs internet and a compatible provider account. Offline does not mean that cloud AI runs on your device.

ChatGPT plugin and older hosted editions

The ChatGPT plugin is separate from the local launcher. Content explicitly sent to an EZ Quiz tool passes through the hosting service and ChatGPT’s infrastructure. The current plugin’s native quiz player uses questions authored by ChatGPT; older generation endpoints may call the configured AI provider. Hosting logs and platform retention follow those services’ policies.

Older hosted async generation can temporarily persist generation jobs and quiz material in its job store. The standalone edition does not use that store or those endpoints. Claims about local-only storage in this guide apply to the standalone path, not every historical deployment.

Feedback and external links

Standalone feedback opens a draft in your email application; you choose whether to send it. Sent feedback may remain in the maintainer’s mailbox. Older hosted feedback forms use a server endpoint. Provider consoles, GitHub, ChatGPT, and the support link are external services with their own policies. Visiting the hosted website also creates ordinary hosting connection metadata.

Keep a copy and stay in control

Use the app without AI, export your quizzes, forget a key, or clear site data. AI output can contain mistakes; check the questions and answers against your study material. The application’s local controls do not determine provider retention.
